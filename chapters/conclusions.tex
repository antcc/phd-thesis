\note{Similar analyses for other functional regression models are carried out for example by \citet{lian2016posterior, abraham2020posterior}, albeit not within a \acs{RKHS} framework.}

Posterior consistency of the Bayes factor in a point-null testing situation was established by \citet{dass2004note}, but their abstract approach cannot provide posterior contraction rates. As a novel contribution, we show that under \(H_0\) the posterior probability of the alternative goes to zero at a polynomial rate, while under \(H_1\) the posterior probability of the null decays exponentially fast.

We believe that this Bayesian framework opens several compelling paths for future research, demonstrating the practical and theoretical utility of \acs{RKHS} representations in managing the inherent infinite-dimensionality of functional data. In particular, exploring the role of impact points within the \acs{RKHS} approximation could yield meaningful new methodologies for change-point detection or variable selection in functional estimation. Furthermore, to our knowledge, the trans-dimensional aspect of the posterior approximation through \acs{RJMCMC}---together with previous work by \citet{berrendero2025bayesian}---is a novel contribution in functional data.
